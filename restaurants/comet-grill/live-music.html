CHARLOTTE'S STAGE FOR LIVE MUSIC

Since 1996, Comet Grill has been Dilworth's home for authentic live music. Every night of the week, local and regional songwriters, blues artists, acoustic duos, and rock bands take our stage — no ticket or cover charge required.

The Stage Rules

WHAT TO EXPECT AT A SHOW

🎟️

Never A Cover Charge

Music should be accessible to the neighborhood. Walk in, grab a stool or a booth, order a cold beer, and enjoy the music without paying admission at the door.

⏰

Nightly 8:30 PM Start

Music typically kicks off around 8:30 PM to 9:00 PM every night of the week. Perfect timing after dinner or for late-night music lovers.

🎸

Eclectic Local Talent

From veteran Charlotte blues legends to emerging acoustic songwriters and high-energy indie rock trios. The sound rotates daily.
