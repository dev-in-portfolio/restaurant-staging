THE STORY OF COMET GRILL

How a small brick bar on Park Road became Charlotte's beloved sanctuary for honest food, cold drinks, and zero-cover live music.

Soul Over Sanitization

A REAL NEIGHBORHOOD BAR

In a city that rapidly transforms, Comet Grill stands as an anchor of authenticity. Located in the Dilworth neighborhood at 2224 Park Road, Comet hasn't tried to become a trendy lounge or a sterile corporate chain.

From the wooden bar counters and sticker-covered taps to the stage light glow and the sizzle of griddled burgers, Comet Grill represents Charlotte at its relaxed, friendly best.

Milestones

OUR TIMELINE

1996 — The Foundation

John & Jeanie Wicker Open Comet Grill

John and Jeanie Wicker opened Comet Grill with a clear vision: a neighborhood gathering place serving great burgers, cold beer, and live music every single night without charging a cover at the door.

2008 — Continuing the Legacy

Tommy Noblett Stewards the Vibe

Tommy Noblett took over management in 2008, preserving the unpretentious atmosphere, supporting local musicians, and solidifying Comet's status as a Charlotte music staple.

Present — Today's Comet

Cora Urso Curates the Next Chapter

Under Cora Urso's leadership, Comet Grill remains true to its core identity: award-winning burgers, Charlottean Shake wings, daily specials, an active patio, and nightly live music for regulars and newcomers alike.
